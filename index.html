Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install FFmpeg manually?

No! Mooze uses a built-in module to fetch a localized version of FFmpeg directly into your Python environment if it doesn't detect one on your system.

Where do my downloaded songs go?

You can set your custom download path directly inside the Download Settings tab in the terminal interface.

Are Spotify downloads 320kbps?

Mooze scrapes the highest possible quality audio stream natively available on YouTube's backend to match your Spotify query, embedding high-res album art directly onto the file.
